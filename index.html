👅 Taste (Rasa)

  • Sweet: Manis (Candy, Sugar)
  • Sour: Asam (Lemon, Mango)
  • Salty: Asin (Salt, Sea Water)
  • Bitter: Pahit (Coffee, Medicine)
  • Spicy: Pedas (Chili)

💪 Body Parts

  • Eyes: Mata (To see)
  • Nose: Hidung (To smell)
  • Ears: Telinga (To listen)
  • Mouth: Mulut (To speak/eat)
  • Hand/Feet: Tangan/Kaki
  • Stomach: Perut

🏥 Health (Sakit)

  • Headache: Sakit Kepala
  • Toothache: Sakit Gigi
  • Stomachache: Sakit Perut
  • Earache: Sakit Telinga

📝 Past Tense

Digunakan untuk masa lampau (Yesterday, Last Week).

Rumus: Subject + Verb 2

Contoh: I went to the zoo.

🔥 Irregular Verbs

  • Go ➡ Went (Pergi)
  • Eat ➡ Ate (Makan)
  • Drink ➡ Drank (Minum)
  • Buy ➡ Bought (Beli)
  • Sleep ➡ Slept (Tidur)
  • Swim ➡ Swam (Berenang)
  • Make ➡ Made (Membuat)

📚 Regular & Vocab

  • Play ➡ Played (Main)
  • Visit ➡ Visited (Berkunjung)
  • Study ➡ Studied (Belajar)
  • Future: Masa Depan
  • Chef: Works in Restaurant
